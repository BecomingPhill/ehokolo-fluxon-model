Understanding EFM Covalent Spacing & Specific Phase Friction

Unlike standard quantum chemical software that models electron shielding and Pauli exclusion via computationally heavy Slater determinants (O(N!)), the **Eholoko Fluxon Model (EFM)** represents chemistry as continuous scalar field thermodynamics. Covalent bonds form as stable, harmonic resonances of overlapping waves.

When a ligand fits correctly in the target active site, the total field gradient flattens out, minimizing the Specific Phase Friction (Espec): Espec = ∑ |∇ψ|2∑ ρ. If a mutation clashes sterically with the pocket wall (Mutation B), the phase windings compound, creating a sharp spike in local gradient energy (Pauli phase repulsion) resulting in a positive binding affinity (ΔE > 0).

Translation Bridge

This bridge translates the physics of the Eholoko Fluxon Model into terms commonly used in traditional molecular docking and biophysics:

EFM Physics Term Biochemical Equivalent Simulation Role
Harmonic Density State (HDS) Atomic Shell Density Zones Discrete wave densities governing electron/nuclear shells.
HDS 1 (Core) Atomic Nucleus & Inner Shells High-density zone, highly repulsive (Pauli repulsion).
HDS 2 (Mantle) Valence Shell / Covalent Bond Shared orbital resonance creating stable bonds.
HDS 3 (Binding) Electrostatic / Hydrogen Bonds Low-density tails governing hydrogen & weak interactions.
Specific Phase Friction (Espec) Wave Phase Gradient Energy Measures how smoothly ligand & pocket waves overlap.
Friction Shift (ΔE) Relative Binding Energy / Score ΔE < 0 indicates stable binding; ΔE > 0 indicates steric clash.
Verlet Wave Relaxation Geometry Optimization Iterative energy minimization to settle wave fields.

Understanding Parameters

  • Grid Resolution: Higher value = higher detail for small molecules. Lower value = faster runtimes.
  • Box Size: The simulated volume (in Angstroms) centered on the active site pocket.
  • Simulation Steps: How long the wave fields are allowed to relax. Increase if metrics fluctuate.
  • Damping (delta): Controls kinetic energy dissipation to settle waves into a ground state.
